Seminar Series • Machine Learning & Optimization

Monthly Seminar

This monthly seminar occurs every first Wednesday of each month, gathering students and researchers from Montréal's ecosystem in machine learning and optimization. The goal is simple: talk about an exiting paper you wrote or read recently. The seminar is open to everyone, but requires registration.

    Coming to the seminar

    950 Av. Beaumont

    Sessions are held in Room 2055 of the IVADO building, 950 Av. Beaumont, on the MIL campus. The simplest way in is the metro: take the blue line to Acadie, leave through the Beaumont exit, and the building is a three-minute walk north-east along the avenue. Buses 16, 92 and 179 stop at that same corner.
